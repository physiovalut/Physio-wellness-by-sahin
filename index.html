FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is one physiotherapy session?

Standard appointment slots are arranged in 30-minute intervals.

Do I need to select my exact diagnosis?

No. If you are unsure about your condition, select “Other / Not Listed” or “General Physiotherapy” and describe your problem in the additional details box.

Is home physiotherapy available?

Home Physiotherapy is available as a service option. Please mention it while requesting an appointment.

How do I request an appointment?

Fill in the appointment form and select your treatment or condition. The details will be prepared for WhatsApp.

Ready to take the next step?

Choose your treatment or condition and request your preferred appointment time.
